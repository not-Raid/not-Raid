## What I Do

I build custom electronics and mess around with microcontrollers. I like turning ideas into real physical projects—from designing custom boards to making controllers talk to hardware over Wi-Fi.

- **Favorite Hardware:** ESP32
- **Go-To Languages:** C++ & Python
- **Focus:** Custom PCB builds, OLED integration, and real-time hardware projects

---

## Projects

### 📟 ESP32 OLED Prototyping Shield
> A custom board made for the ESP32 DevKit 1. Plug-and-play headers mapped out to drive 0.96" OLED displays without messing around with jumper wires.

### 🎮 PS4 Controller Wi-Fi Link
> A project that grabs live button and analog stick inputs from a PS4 controller and streams the data straight to an ESP32 over local Wi-Fi.
